Scope & Requirements

Clear expectations before the project starts.

Each project follows an agreed scope, revision limit, and client requirements to keep the work focused and predictable.

Revisions

One revision round means one consolidated batch of client feedback. Revisions may include text, images, colors, spacing, and minor UI adjustments within the agreed project scope.

Additional Work

New pages, new sections, new features, major redesigns, concept changes, or new requirements are considered additional work and are not counted as regular revisions.

Deployment Assistance

One deployment assistance is included for a compatible client-owned hosting platform, including basic configuration and a final check after deployment.

What You Provide

  • Final text and website content
  • Logo and branding assets, if available
  • Images and supporting assets
  • Contact and social media links
  • References and special requirements
  • Hosting or domain access when deployment is required

Not Included

  • Copywriting from scratch
  • Logo or brand identity creation
  • Custom backend or database
  • Authentication or login system
  • Admin dashboard or CMS
  • E-commerce or payment system
  • Complex API or third-party integrations
  • Hosting and domain fees
  • Ongoing maintenance
